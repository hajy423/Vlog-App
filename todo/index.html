            Daily nudge

            One repeating calendar alert, every day, forever. It's the backstop for everything on the list that has no time of its own — and it carries your streak, so the number you're protecting is the first thing you see.

            Turning it off here stops Nudge tracking it — delete the repeating event in Calendar to actually silence it.

            How this works

            1
            Get it out of your head, instantly

            Type it, or tap 🎙 and say it — "call mum tomorrow at six" sets the reminder in the same breath. Routines work too: "gym every monday at 7am". New things land in Later, not in your face.

            2
            Pick three. That's the day.

            Each morning you choose up to three things — the first one is the one that matters. Finish them and the app tells you you're done. Not "here's forty more". Done.

            3
            Reminders come to you

            Timed tasks are handed to your phone's Calendar, which raises the alert on your lock screen — app closed, phone in pocket. The Home Screen icon carries a badge with your open count.

            4
            Finish anything, keep the flame

            🔥 counts days in a row you completed something. One small win protects it. Miss a whole day and it resets — that's the deal.

            5
            Nothing rots here

            Things left sitting get commented on — and after two weeks untouched, the app stops nagging and asks one question: still matters, or let it go? A list you're not scared to open is one you'll actually use.

            Everything stays on this device. No account, no server, no sync. Add it to your Home Screen (Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen) for the badge and full-screen app. "Hey Siri, add to Nudge" setup is in the README.
