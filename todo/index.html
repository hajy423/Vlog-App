      How this reaches you

      1
      Calendar does the shouting

      A website can't raise an alert on a closed iPhone. Calendar can, and it's always running. Give a to-do a time and Nudge hands it over as a calendar alert — the reminder that lights up your lock screen says the task itself, so you never have to open anything.

      2
      The icon keeps score

      Add Nudge to your Home Screen and its icon carries a red badge with the number of open items. It sits there every time you unlock your phone, no notification required.

      3
      Put it where your thumb already goes

      In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Drop the icon in your dock, next to the apps you do open.
